Room 3 — Descent Mission

You are parachuting down from an American transport aircraft, heading straight into the Syrian base where the Russian submarine is hidden. Your ultimate mission: once inside the submarine, you must retrieve the encrypted key locked deep within its hull.

But first — if you choose to accept this mission — your task in this room is to detect anomalous signals from aircraft crossing your path during descent. Identify which ones belong to Russian enemy forces and which ones are allied. Your survival, and the mission’s success, depends on your accuracy.

Detect anomalous flight paths
